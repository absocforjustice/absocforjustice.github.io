Background

A university tied to the arms trade

Freedom of Information disclosures analysed by Demilitarise Education show that City St George’s has 44 direct links with arms-sector companies — partnerships, research funding, consultancy, PhD studentships and tuition sponsorships — worth around £4.5 million over eight years, including more than £3.6 million in arms-sector research funding alone.

Its partners include BAE Systems, Boeing, Airbus, Rolls-Royce, Thales and the UK’s Defence Science and Technology Laboratory. Between 2013 and 2025, arms-sector partners disclosed more than £4.9 million in funding, much of it in the School of Science and Technology — in direct contradiction of the university’s own investment policy.

These companies make and supply weapons used to kill civilians in Palestine and Lebanon. A university that profits from them is not neutral. We call on City St George’s to disclose every link, divest from every arms company, and end its research partnerships with weapons makers.
